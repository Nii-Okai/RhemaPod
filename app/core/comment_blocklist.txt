# Sermon-comment objectionable-content floor (plan §A.7, D3).
#
# One term or phrase per line. Lines starting with '#' and blank lines are
# ignored. Matching runs on the NORMALISED comment (NFKC, Cyrillic/Greek
# look-alikes folded to Latin, case-folded, diacritics and zero-width/bidi
# characters removed) on WHOLE-WORD boundaries, so a term never fires inside a
# larger benign word.
#
# Line syntax:
#   term      exact whole word or phrase
#   +stem     profanity stem, also matches its inflections as whole words:
#             -s -es -ed -ing -y -er -ers, a doubled final consonant
#             (shit -> shitting) and -y -> -ies (pussy -> pussies)
#   !phrase   self-harm incitement, matched with whitespace and punctuation
#             collapsed and repeated letters squeezed ("killyourself",
#             "kill ur self", "go dieee", "only fans", "n u d e s"), still on word
#             boundaries at both ends. Also matched after a leetspeak fold
#             (0->o 1->i 3->e 4->a @->a $->s): "kill y0urself", "k1ll urself".
#
# This is a deliberate minimal seed, reviewed by Isaac at gate C4. It is a
# floor, not a filter of record: report + moderator hide remain the main
# defence. Terms that are legitimate in scripture or sermon discussion
# (bastard, whore, damn, hell, cock, rape, porn, sex, ass) are intentionally NOT
# listed. Leetspeak and spaced-out letters of profanity are accepted residuals.
# (The leetspeak fold applies to the ! entries only.)
